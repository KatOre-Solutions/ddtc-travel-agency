# DDTC destination photography

The images in `public/images/` are real destination reference photographs downloaded from Wikimedia Commons on 2 November 2026. They are representative destination imagery, not evidence of a DDTC trip, accommodation booking, included attraction, or particular vessel. The promotional posters in `events/` were not modified or used as website imagery.

Photographs were downloaded at a web-sized resolution supplied by Wikimedia; no additional image editing was performed. Responsive website presentation may crop the visible area. Where applicable, these resized/cropped presentations remain available under the source image's stated Creative Commons license. No photographer or source endorses DDTC.

Include a publicly accessible photo credits link in the website footer. The machine-readable credits are in `public/image-credits.json`; the local research records are in `docs/assets/`.

## Content boundaries

- The cruise photograph was taken in Greece. Use only to illustrate cruising generally, with a representative-image caption. It does not show a South African sailing or confirm the ship for any DDTC experience.
- The Lesotho photograph shows mountains in a non-snow season. It must not imply snow conditions or depict the advertised ski resort.
- The Mozambique photograph depicts Bazaruto Island. It must not be identified as Maputo, Ponta do Ouro, or an included tour location unless confirmed separately.
- The Bali temple photograph illustrates Bali; its presence does not add that temple to an itinerary.
- The Johannesburg photograph depicts the city skyline, not Gold Reef City. Use a city caption.
- Some images show people in public places. Do not imply that they are DDTC clients or testimonial authors.

All selected photographs were visually inspected. Ambiguous locations, a Tasmania photo returned for Clifton, grayscale Durban imagery, and lower-quality trial candidates were excluded.
