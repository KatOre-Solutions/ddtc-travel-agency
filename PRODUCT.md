# DDTC Travel Agency

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Stack
Next.js App Router, TypeScript and Tailwind CSS, as requested in the approved implementation brief. Local development first; prepared for Vercel. No deployment authorized.

## Users
Individuals, couples, families, social groups and corporate travellers looking for accessible, thoughtfully planned holidays.

## Product Purpose
Introduce DDTC, present its actual advertised experiences and generate qualified travel enquiries. Success means a visitor can find trip information, choose a package and contact the agency.

## Positioning
Premium presentation. Accessible experiences. A Rabokala-based business operating since February 2020, with planned local and international holidays and cruise assistance.

## Operating Context
Ten client promotional posters are reference material in events/. Prices, terms and availability require reconfirmation. Preserve the files. Cape Town's road and flight packages must remain distinct.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Enquiry website only. No payments, binding reservations, customer accounts, invented availability, invented reviews or unverified MSC partnership claims. Email submission requires configured credentials; WhatsApp is the functional alternative. Legal and review approval remain open.

## Brand Commitments
Approved minimal dark cinematic DDTC identity. Fixed navy palette and fonts from DDTC-WEBSITE-BRIEF.md. Original poster layouts are excluded from the interface. The client subsequently selected the supplied primary logo with serif navy DDTC letters, gold sunrise, palms, blue mountains and waves, aircraft, full company name and motto. Preserve that artwork; use its white/gold adaptation on navy and compact traced wordmark on mobile. Raster-derived vectors still require professional artwork review.

## Evidence on Hand
DDTC-WEBSITE-BRIEF.md and ten poster files. Review meanings are provided, but original quotations, names and publication permission are absent. Photography will be licensed destination imagery, not represented as photos of completed DDTC trips.

## Product Principles
Use supplied facts. Represent uncertainty honestly. Make planning accessible. Keep enquiries clear. Separate content from reusable presentation.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Responsive mobile-first web, keyboard access, reduced motion, clear validation, and WCAG 2.2 AA as the implementation target.
